# PMVS review record

This record tracks focused technical and editorial passes over the pre-EIP proposal. It is design history, not normative text. A later pass may supersede an earlier result; the current specification, profiles, schemas, and vectors are authoritative. After each prose pass, the text was checked with Orwell Writing, No AI Slop, and Humanizer, in that order. The automated prose check rejects the clearest stock terms and em or en dashes.

## Boundary of this review

The repository is a production-oriented proposal, schema, and vector suite. It does not certify a deployment. L2 and L3 claims are unavailable in this draft because PMVS-M1 lacks a closed complete compute profile and a standalone engine that derives the full valuation output. Even an L1 production claim still needs the bounded request-remedy profile, complete implementation code, two independent verifiers, independent security review of the anchor and settlement contracts, venue-data rights review, legal review, and an observed run beginning with a conforming component genesis.
